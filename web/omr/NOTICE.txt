John's sheet-music reader (Import song: images and PDFs, web version)
https://github.com/Thinato/john - John itself is under the MIT licence (LICENSE in the repository).

The reader runs in your browser; the image never leaves your device. It is made of the software and model below.
Paths are relative to this file (omr/ on the site).

copisteria, by KobiMusic - Apache License 2.0
  This product includes software developed by KobiMusic (https://kobi.music):
  copisteria, https://github.com/kobimusic/copisteria
  copisteria/ is the JavaScript port of copisteria's reader (copisteria commit
  c685f280914d91a694494588d9f6a376d7917eba) made in MusicOCR and its fork brunombo/OpticalMusicRecognition
  (https://github.com/brunombo/OpticalMusicRecognition, commit 93ee338c196d2aef0d8eccfb86c4cec8446e743f): translated
  from Python to JavaScript, the OCR-text features left out, fingerings.js added. John changed write.js: every line
  after the first starts with <print new-system="yes"/>.
  Licence: licenses/copisteria-Apache-2.0.txt. Notice: licenses/copisteria-NOTICE.txt.

copista-2m model, by KobiMusic - Apache License 2.0
  This product includes the copista-2m model, developed by KobiMusic (https://kobi.music):
  https://huggingface.co/kobimusic/copista-2m
  models/ holds its weights converted from PyTorch to ONNX, as published in
  https://huggingface.co/pauldubois98/musicocr-models (commit 049d45cf85545b169392bc4376b7cf0570408f44), mirrored
  unchanged in John's release https://github.com/Thinato/john/releases/tag/models-v1.
  Notices: licenses/copista-2m-NOTICE.txt, licenses/copista-models-NOTICE.txt (the ONNX export).
  Licence: licenses/copista-models-LICENSE.txt.
